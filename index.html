02 / Behind the lens

More than just the final score.

What once started as a hobby has grown into the start of something bigger. The VZN is my way of capturing football beyond the result — the passion, the pressure and the people who make the game what it is.

From capturing late winners, cupsets and everything in between, my aim is to tell the story of every match through the moments that might otherwise be missed.

Whether it's the intensity of a match, the celebrations after the whistle or the people working behind the scenes, there's always another side to the game worth capturing.

This is football through my lens. This is The VZN.
