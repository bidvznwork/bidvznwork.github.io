Behind The Lens

More Than
A Game.

I'm Brady, better known as BIDVZN. Since leaving my academic years behind me, photography has become my life. What was once a hobby is now the start of a career I'm determined to build. From capturing late winners, cupsets and everything inbetween, I aim to tell the story of the match through my lens.

I've worked with clubs including Punjab United, SE Dons, Hashtag United and Croydon Athletic, through these opportunities I've developed my style and experience in football media.
